**CONTEXT**: 
We are building a community open-source plugin for OpenCode: `opencode-plugin-mempalace`. 
This plugin integrates the "MemPalace" (an AI memory system based on ChromaDB and SQLite) into OpenCode's terminal assistant.
We have already completed the architecture design and feasibility research.
